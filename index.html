Microphone

If a Bluetooth device (AirPods, headphones, car, speaker) is connected, iOS may record from its microphone, even if it's across the room. Choose iPhone Microphone here. The list fills in after your first recording.

Voice

All texts, phrases and drills are pre-recorded with neural voices that take turns: Heart & Michael (US) and Emma & George (UK). The Apple voice below is used only for phrases you add yourself.

Note: iOS only lets web apps use some of its voices. Enhanced and Premium voices you downloaded in iPhone Settings usually don't appear here; that's an Apple restriction. Try a few voices from the list and keep the one that sounds best.
